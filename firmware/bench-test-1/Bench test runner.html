Bench test 1

This page runs the whole test for you. It changes the screen color, sends the commands to the board, and tells you when to move things. Your only job is to hold the sensor and turn the samples.

Before you start

  1. Turn the room lights off, so the screen is the only light. (Optional: a short ring of dark paper taped around the sensor chip blocks stray light even better.)
  2. Draw a small straight line with a marker (or stick a strip of tape) along one edge of the sunglasses lens and of the peeled cell. This is the "mark" you will point at each angle.
  3. Set the screen brightness to about three quarters.
  4. Make this browser window full screen with F11.

What to test

During the test: Space skips the wait, P pauses, Esc stops.
